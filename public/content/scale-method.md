# The Scale Method

We spent over a decade perfecting the exact six steps it takes to scale a creative
agency, creating impact, providing freedom and maximizing profits.

1. **Positioning** — where the agency sits in its market.
2. **Expertize** — the depth that justifies the position.
3. **Specialization** — narrowing to what compounds.
4. **Authority** — being the reference in that niche.
5. **Seamless Operation** — delivery that runs without the owner.
6. **Recurning Revenue** — income that recurs instead of restarting each month.

> Note: "Recurning" and "Expertize" are spelled as they appear in the source design.
